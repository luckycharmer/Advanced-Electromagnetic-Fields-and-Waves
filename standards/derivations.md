# Lecture-kit derivation standard

Apply to future PHY368, PHY709 and PHY765 lecture kits unless superseded by an explicit instruction.

Include a separate handwritten-style derivation PDF and retain a typeset, editable equation source.
Use a legible handwriting font for Latin equation letters, explanatory notes and labels; preserve standard mathematical glyphs where the font lacks coverage.
Show the starting law, assumptions, geometry, notation, intermediate algebra/calculus, boxed or clearly isolated result, units and a limiting or numerical check.
For interface problems declare the normal before writing any jump condition. Include vector sketches and sign checks.
Derivation content must cover the equations actually taught; avoid decorative pages without instructional steps.
Keep student derivation pages separate from instructor answers when an activity requires withholding the result.
Check mathematical correctness, font/glyph rendering and page layout before delivery. Do not present the simulated handwriting as the instructor's actual handwriting.

For 15 October 2026, use the twelve-page derivation booklet alongside the 150-minute instructor lecture source. The booklet includes a wave-equation preview; it does not replace the full next lecture.
