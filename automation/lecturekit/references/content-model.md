# One content master

master.json is authoritative. Generated Markdown, TeX, PDF and PPTX are exports. Use stable alphanumeric/hyphen/underscore IDs. Equation/constant tokens `{{eq:EQ1}}` and `{{const:C1}}` expand from central records. Figures use relative paths within the master directory; escaping paths/symlinks are blocked.

Fields: meta (course/session/topic/date/duration/level/timezone); context (official syllabus, prerequisites, textbook edition, previous teaching, next plan, assessment coverage and destinations with evidence/verified flags); sources (IDs, URI, edition, actual access, snapshot/hash); claims (scope/source/evidence); outcomes (measurable statements/syllabus mapping); constants (value/unit/source/tolerance); equations (LaTeX, symbols, assumptions, geometry, intermediate steps, units and verification); figures (source/editable source, alt, attribution/licence/public permission); blocks (Markdown/outcomes/sources/claims/figures and kind); questions (stage/prompt/outcomes/marks/writing space/solution/marking/common slips/active assessment); documents (purpose/block IDs/question IDs/public approval); slides (IDs/title/block IDs/questions/reveals/private notes); timeline (minutes/type/outcomes/slides/attempted questions/cut priority); interactive (benefit/source/fallback/tests or not-needed reason); policy (public allowlist/disclosure/access).

Required document purposes: preview, reference, worksheet, exit, followup, derivations, teaching_plan, solutions. Every question has one central solution. Student documents insert prompts only; instructor solutions expand the corresponding key. Worked examples are instructional answers and remain allowed. Student decks exclude private notes and reveal slides. Classroom reveals may use attempted worksheet/diagnostic/follow-up questions, never active exit/assignment questions. The timeline must place attempts before reveals.

Core context/source facts cannot be replaced by unchecked memories. A draft course profile supplies retrieval locators, not verified content. Gate receipts bind master and output manifest digests plus evidence hashes. Changing content or evidence invalidates approval. Review semantic answer leaks as well as structural filters. Legitimate course discussion of AI and mandatory disclosure must survive branding cleanup.

For slides, use central equation tokens or equation_ids, which render from the exact LaTeX record; use Unicode for inline mathematics. Raw TeX in slide body/question text blocks the build until correctly typeset. Figure slide inputs must be PNG exports from retained editable source assets. The default image-based equation in PPTX has an editable .tex source, not a native PowerPoint equation object.
