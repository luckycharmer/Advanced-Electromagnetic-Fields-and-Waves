## Class sequence

0-10 min: diagnostic. 10-30: Maxwell equations. 30-45: charge conservation. 45-65: constitutive response. 65-75: guided dielectric calculation. 75-85: break. 85-110: boundary derivations. 110-130: worked problems. 130-142: conductor and wave connection. 142-150: exit check.
