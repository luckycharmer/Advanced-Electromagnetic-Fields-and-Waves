Primary text: Jackson, Classical Electrodynamics, 3rd edition. Use Chapter 6 for Maxwell equations and charge conservation, Chapter 4 for dielectric response, and Chapter 5 for magnetic media. Chapter 7 provides the subsequent plane-wave/interface connection. Chapter references are topic mappings.

Supplement: MIT OpenCourseWare 6.641, graduate course, Spring 2005. Review lectures L1-L3 and L7-L8. [Lecture notes](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-641-electromagnetic-fields-forces-and-motion-spring-2005/pages/lecture-notes/).

Boundary-condition supplement: MIT 6.013, Lecture 2, Media and Boundary Conditions. [Lecture PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-013-electromagnetics-and-applications-spring-2009/0ec2c39ffccb37e83b43f6f59868f8d7_MIT6_013S09_lec02.pdf).

Alternative explanation: Richard Fitzpatrick, University of Texas at Austin, [Classical Electromagnetism](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/jk1/Electromagnetism/index.html), sections on Maxwell equations and dielectric/magnetic media.

Before class: revise divergence and Stokes theorems, curl-free electrostatics, normal/tangential components, and Cartesian cross products.

After class: reproduce the four interface proofs and complete the assigned original problem set.
